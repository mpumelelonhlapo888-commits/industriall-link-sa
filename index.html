How it works

From enquiry to introduction.

No material changes hands through this website. The parties discuss terms and verify each other before any transaction.

STEP 01

Submit an enquiry

Share the material type, quantity, location and whether you are buying or supplying.

STEP 02

We review the details

We check whether the request is clear and identify potential matching businesses.

STEP 03

Make an introduction

With permission, we connect the parties so they can assess quality, pricing and logistics.

STEP 04

What makes a good enquiry?

The more detail you provide, the easier it is to assess a potential match.

  • Exact material and grade, if known
  • Approximate quantity and frequency
  • Collection suburb / town
  • Photos and contamination details
  • Whether you can load or deliver
  • Target price or buying requirements

Important questions

Before any material changes hands.

Does IndustrialLink collect or transport waste?

No. The launch model is a referral and sourcing service. We do not collect, transport, store, process or dispose of materials. The parties must arrange these activities with appropriately authorised operators.

Are all waste materials accepted?

No. Material type, quality, quantity, contamination, legal classification, permits and buyer requirements must be checked. Hazardous and healthcare waste are excluded from the initial service.

Are buyers and suppliers verified?

This is an early-stage network. Do not assume a listed or introduced party is verified. Independently check company identity, references, authorisations, site conditions, material classification and payment terms before proceeding.

How does IndustrialLink earn money?

Potential revenue includes a sourcing fee or a referral/brokerage fee under a written agreement. Fees are not automatic and are only payable on agreed terms. No transaction or income is guaranteed.

What happens to my information?

Enquiry information should only be used to respond and arrange a requested introduction. Before public launch, publish a complete POPIA privacy notice, secure the form destination, set a retention period and provide a way to request access or deletion.
